# Coconut Banana Ice Cream

## Ingredients

- 2 ripe bananas, peeled and sliced
- 1 can (about 1 1/2 cups) full-fat coconut milk
- 1 tbsp honey or maple syrup (optional)
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- Pinch of cinnamon

## Instructions

- Freeze banana slices for at least 2 hours.
- In a blender, combine frozen bananas, coconut milk, honey, vanilla, and cinnamon.
- Blend until smooth and creamy.
- Pour mixture into an ice cream maker and churn until thick.
- Transfer to a container and freeze for 2–3 hours.
- Scoop and serve chilled.
