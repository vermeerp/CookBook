# Asian Ramen Coleslaw

```
Author: 
Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 12 minutes
Total Time: 27 minutes
Yield: 8-10 servings
```

## Ingredients

### Salad
- 1 (3 oz.) package ramen noodles, crushed (Chicken or Oriental flavor)
- 2 ounces slivered or sliced almonds
- 1 (16 oz.) package shredded coleslaw mix
- 1 tablespoon butter (optional, for toasting)
- Salt to taste (optional, for toasting)

### Dressing
- 1/2 cup white sugar
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil (corn oil preferred)
- 1/4 cup cider vinegar
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce (low sodium version recommended)
- 1/2 packet ramen seasoning (reserved from the ramen package)

## Instructions

- Crush the ramen noodles into small pieces while still in the bag. Open the bag and break up any remaining large pieces.
- Mix the crushed ramen and almonds and spread in a pile on a cookie sheet.
- Optional: Add a 1 tablespoon pat of butter to the pile of noodles and a sprinkle of salt.
- In a preheated 350°F oven, toast the crushed noodles and nuts on a cookie sheet until golden brown, about 10-12 minutes. Stir once or twice while baking if using butter.
- To prepare the dressing, whisk together the sugar, oil, vinegar, soy sauce, and ramen seasoning. The dressing is much better if prepared beforehand and refrigerated overnight. Shake well before serving as the sugar settles.
- Just before serving, combine the coleslaw, toasted ramen noodles and nuts in a serving bowl.
- Pour the dressing over the salad, toss, and serve.

## Notes

- Easiest to crush ramen noodles while still in the bag - break up with hands, then open the bag to avoid mess.
- The butter and salt step for toasting is optional but does make the roasted bits taste better.
- If not serving immediately, don't add the toasted bits or dressing until the very last minute to keep them crunchy.
- This dressing does not mix easily due to the sugar content. Best to shake it up in a plastic container with a lid.
- This recipe makes more dressing than the salad really needs. You might want to hold some in reserve before using the whole mixture on the salad.
- Traveling Tips: This salad is great for potlucks because it's very portable. Roast the ramen and nuts ahead of time and store in a ziplock bag or container. Mix the dressing and refrigerate in a small container (glass jar works great). Toss the unopened bag of coleslaw mix in your cooler with the dressing, don't forget the ramen mix, and take your empty serving bowl and spoon with you. Nothing to spill or spoil. Mix it all on site.
