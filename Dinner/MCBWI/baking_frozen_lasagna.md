# Baking Lasagna from Frozen

## General Guidelines

For baking a frozen lasagna, you'll generally need to add about 45-60 minutes to the normal baking time.

## Instructions for Easiest Spinach Lasagna (from frozen)

### Baking Time
- Keep it covered with foil and bake at 400°F for about 60-75 minutes
- Remove the foil and bake for another 20-25 minutes until the cheese is golden and bubbly
- **Total time: roughly 80-100 minutes** (compared to 55 minutes for fresh)

### Tips
- Don't thaw it first - bake straight from frozen to prevent it from getting watery
- Check the center with a knife or thermometer to make sure it's heated through (165°F internal temperature)
- You might want to add 5-10 extra minutes to the resting time since it'll be very hot
- Alternative: Bake covered at 375°F for the entire time if you're worried about the top browning too much - just add about 15-20 minutes to the overall time

## General Rule of Thumb
- Add 45-60 minutes to the original baking time when cooking from frozen
- Always bake covered for the majority of the time to prevent over-browning
- Remove foil for the last 20-25 minutes to brown the top
